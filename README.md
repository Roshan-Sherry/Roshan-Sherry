## 📄 Publications

**Enhancing Academic Pathway Planning through Knowledge Graphs**  
**R. Sherry**, R. Thomas, V. Rani, N. T. Bhuvan, R. Latip, B. Paul  
*Springer LNNS (forthcoming) · Presented at ICIDS 2025, Manipal University Jaipur*  
KnowUNI: extracts course prerequisites with NLP, models them as a Neo4j knowledge graph, and recommends courses via cosine similarity (97% accuracy vs. 83–91% for tree-based baselines).

**Comprehensive Benchmarking of Parallel Programming Models: A Multi-Dimensional Performance Analysis**  
A. Mishra, A. Priya, S. Niranjana, A. S. George, N. T. Bhuvan, **R. Sherry**  
*ICoICI 2025 (IEEE), pp. 1452–1458*  
Compares ten models (OpenMP, MPI, CUDA, OpenCL, Pthreads, TBB, Charm++, HPX, Kokkos, SYCL) on speedup, scalability, memory use and energy efficiency.

**Smart Job Application Assistant: A Cognitive Job Recommendation System using NLP and Machine Learning**  
**R. Sherry**, F. Rahman, D. Sarah, N. T. Bhuvan, A. S.  
*Springer LNNS · Presented at ICCCT 2025, NFSU Goa & Florida International University*  
Resume parsing, multi-label job classification (TF-IDF + logistic regression vs. RoBERTa), and Sentence-Transformer ranking of scraped job postings.
